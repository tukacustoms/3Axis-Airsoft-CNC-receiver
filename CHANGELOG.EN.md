### Changed

* Adjusted the rear wiring spacing to make it wider and closer to the stock attachment position
* Revised the stock geometry and machining approach, adding central slots based on machine constraints

### Fixed

* Middle gearbox pin adjusted by -0.35 mm on the Z axis; it needed to sit slightly lower, from 21.65 mm to 21.95 mm
* Front upper-receiver attachment adjusted from 14.00 mm to 14.50 mm ± 0.50 mm
* Alignment of the Magwell internal structure, 0.5mm unaligned to the left
* Extended Wire port on the back to 15mm x 10mm instead of 10mm x 10mm

## [V1.1] - Prototype

### Added

* Full split receiver system
* Lower receiver manufacturing

### Issues Found

* Rear wiring outlet too small and positioned too low
* Side slots of the stock attachment too wide
* Front pin spacing too tight
* Trigger guard fitment too tight
* No chamfer around the magwell
* Bolt alignment tolerance too tight
